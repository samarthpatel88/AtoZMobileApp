AtoZ Kids Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Our promise

AtoZ Kids is a learning app for young children. It works completely offline and does not collect, store or share any personal information about you or your child.

No internet, no tracking

The app does not ask for internet access. It has no advertising, no analytics, no accounts, no push notifications and no third-party services. Nothing leaves your device.

What stays on your device

The app remembers only your settings (sound, music and voice choice), the stars your child has earned, their best quiz scores and the letters and numbers they have traced. This is stored privately on the device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app does not need any permissions. When a grown-up chooses Share or Contact us, your device's own sharing or e-mail app opens. These two buttons are protected by a simple question for grown-ups.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no data at all, we never knowingly collect information from children. The app is designed to be suitable for children and to comply with children's privacy rules such as COPPA and GDPR-K.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear here in the app with a new date.
