Desi Board Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Desi Board Games (“the app”) is made by AtoZ Kids. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short answer: the app collects no personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Version 1 of the app does not even have internet access.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings (sound, vibration, theme), your game statistics and your remembered game choices.
  • The game you are playing, so you can continue it later.

This data never leaves your phone, except through Android’s own backup if you have turned it on (settings and statistics only, never the game in progress). You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Android may send an anonymous crash report to Google Play, the same as for every app, if you allowed this in your phone’s settings. These reports do not identify you. The app itself sends nothing.

Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations on captures and wins. You can turn this off in Settings.

Children

The app is for players of all ages, including children. It collects no data from anyone, so it collects no data from children.

Future online play

A later version may add online games with friends. Before that happens this policy will be updated to say exactly what is sent and why, and the app will show what changed.
